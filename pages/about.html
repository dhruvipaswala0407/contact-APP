User Guide & Overview

How to Use Your
Digital Contact Book

Designed specifically to upgrade legacy physical contact books into an elegant, fast, and secure digital register.

Quick Start Instructions

1

Adding New Contacts

Click on Add Contact in the navigation bar. Enter the Company Name, Manager Name, Phone Number, and Email Address. Real-time validation checks for proper email syntax and phone lengths.

2

Instant Live Search

Head to the Contacts page. Type any keyword into the search bar. The directory filters simultaneously across company names, managers, phone numbers, and emails with zero lag.

3

Direct Communication

Each contact card features quick-action buttons. Click the phone link to initiate a call, click the email address to compose an email, or hit the copy icon to copy numbers or emails directly to your clipboard.

4

Sorting Your Directory

Sort entries alphabetically by Company (A→Z or Z→A), by Manager Name, or order them by Recently Added to quickly locate partners added this week.

5

Editing & Deleting

Need to update a manager's phone or email? Click Edit on any contact card. When removing an obsolete contact, a stylish confirmation modal ensures accidental clicks never delete records.

6

Data Backup & Privacy

Your business contacts remain safely stored in your browser's private local storage. Click the Export CSV button on the Contacts page anytime to download a backup file for your spreadsheets.

Ready to Explore Your Register?

Your digital register comes pre-seeded with 5 verified corporate sample contacts so you can experience instant search and communication immediately.
