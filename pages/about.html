User Guide & Overview

How to Use Your
Digital Contact Book

Designed specifically to upgrade legacy physical contact books into an elegant, fast, and secure digital register.

Why Switch from Paper to Digital?

Feature Physical Paper Register Digital Contact Book
Search Speed Slow; flipping through pages manually Instant; fast browsing & clean layout
Calling & Emailing Manual dial from paper notes One-click native click-to-call & click-to-email
Form Validation None; duplicate entries and typos often go unnoticed Native HTML5 validation for emails and numbers
Backup & Durability Fragile; vulnerable to loss, water, or physical wear Safe CSV backup file download
Editing Records Messy strikethroughs and correction fluid Clean & seamless update at any moment

How It Works: Quick Start Instructions

1

Adding New Contacts

Click on Add Contact in the navigation bar. Enter the Company Name, Manager Name, Phone Number, and Email Address. Built-in HTML5 validation ensures correct formatting.

2

Unified Directory

Head to the Contacts page. Browse all registered partners with corporate avatars, manager names, and instant contact channels.

3

Direct Communication

Each contact card features quick-action links. Click the phone number to launch your system dialer, or click the email address to open your default email client.

4

Aesthetic Glassmorphic UI

Modern, responsive aurora theme designed with pure CSS. Enjoy fluid responsive layouts, soft ambient lighting, and subtle micro-interactions on any screen.

5

Editing Records

Need to update a manager's phone or email? Click Edit on any contact card to view and revise the company details.

6

Data Backup & CSV

Click the Export CSV button on the Contacts page to immediately download your contact register for spreadsheets or offline archives.
